About Us

A Trusted Name in Deira Gold Souk

Mercy Habesha Gold is a jewelry business rooted in Ethiopian and Habesha heritage, proudly located in the heart of Deira Gold Souk, Dubai. We bring together elegant traditional designs, genuine gold craftsmanship, and warm, personalized customer service.

From everyday pieces to wedding sets and custom creations, every item is chosen and crafted with care — so you can wear your culture with pride, wherever you are.
